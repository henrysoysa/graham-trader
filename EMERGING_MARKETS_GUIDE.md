## 💡 Pro Tips for Emerging Markets

### 1. **Adjust Your Expectations**
Emerging markets are different from developed markets:
- Higher volatility is normal
- P/E ratios may be higher due to growth
- Currency fluctuations add risk/opportunity
- Political risk exists

### 2. **Focus on Quality First**
Look for:
- ✅ Strong balance sheets (Current Ratio > 1.2)
- ✅ Positive earnings for 5+ years
- ✅ Dividend payments (shows commitment to shareholders)
- ✅ Established industry leaders

### 3. **Diversify Across Countries**
Don't put all eggs in one country:
- Spread across 3-5 countries
- Mix regions (Asia + Latin America + Africa)
- Balance sectors (tech, commodities, consumer, finance)

### 4. **Understand Local Factors**
Research before investing:
- Currency trends (strong/weak local currency)
- Political stability
- Trade relationships
- Regulatory environment

### 5. **Use ADRs When Possible**
Benefits of ADRs over local listings:
- Easier to trade through US brokers
- Better price discovery
- More analyst coverage
- USD-denominated (no currency conversion for US investors)

## 🔍 "Under the Radar" Opportunities

### Finding Hidden Gems

To find stocks NOT in major ETFs:

1. **Screen smaller indexes:**
   - Indonesia IDX
   - Vietnam VN30
   - Mexico IPC (smaller cap stocks)

2. **Look for lower market caps:**
   - Sort results by market cap (ascending)
   - Focus on $1B-$10B range
   - These are often overlooked by big funds

3. **Analyze score but not passing:**
   - Stocks scoring 3-4/5 but not passing may be overlooked
   - Check why they didn't pass (might be temporary issue)
   - Could be value opportunities

4. **Compare to ETFs:**
   - Check holdings of EEM (iShares MSCI Emerging Markets)
   - Check VWO (Vanguard FTSE Emerging Markets)
   - Stocks NOT in these may be undervalued

### Currency Considerations
- Local exchange stocks are priced in local currency
- ADRs are USD-denominated
- Currency movements can significantly impact returns
- Strong USD = headwind for local stocks
- Weak USD = tailwind for local stocks
