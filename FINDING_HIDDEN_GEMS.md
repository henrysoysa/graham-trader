## 🎯 What Are "Hidden Gems"?

Hidden gems are stocks that:
- ✅ **NOT in major indexes** (S&P 500, FTSE 100, etc.)
- ✅ **Small/mid-cap** (typically < $10B market cap)
- ✅ **Underfollowed** by analysts and institutions
- ✅ **Meet Graham value criteria** despite being overlooked
- ✅ **Have growth potential** ("up and comers")

These are companies that COULD become the next big thing, but are currently flying under the radar.

## 🔍 How to Find Hidden Gems: Step-by-Step

### **Strategy 1: The "Under $10B Hunt"**

**Goal:** Find ultra-small companies with huge upside potential

1. Open Graham Trader → Stock Screener
2. Select **"Emerging Markets Small Caps 🌟"**
3. Choose **"Enterprising Investor"** strategy
4. Click **"Run Screening"**
5. Select **"All Stocks (Ranked by Score)"**
6. Look at the Market Cap column
7. Focus on stocks with:
   - Market cap < $10B
   - Total score ≥ 4/5
   - P/E < 15
   - P/B < 3

**Why This Works:**
- Market cap < $10B = institutional investors often ignore
- These can 5x-10x if they grow into major indexes
- Still have Graham value characteristics

### **Strategy 2: The "Country-Specific Deep Dive"**

**Goal:** Become an expert in one emerging market

1. **Pick a fast-growing country:**
   - India (demographic dividend, tech hub)
   - Brazil (commodities, agriculture)
   - Indonesia (Southeast Asia growth)
   - Vietnam (manufacturing shift from China)

2. **Screen both large AND small caps:**
   - First: Screen "India - NIFTY 50" (large caps)
   - Then: Screen "India - Mid Caps" (hidden gems)
   - Compare: What sectors are strong in both?

3. **Identify overlooked leaders:**
   - Look for mid-caps in the same sectors as successful large caps
   - These are often "mini versions" of bigger companies
   - Example: If Infosys (large) is strong, look at Mphasis (mid)

### **Strategy 3: The "Contrarian China Play"**

**Goal:** Find undervalued Chinese stocks while others are fearful

1. Screen **"China - Small Cap ADRs"**
2. Look for companies with:
   - P/E < 10 (extremely cheap due to market sentiment)
   - P/B < 2
   - Positive earnings for 5+ years
   - US-listed (easier access)

3. Focus on sectors with:
   - Domestic consumption (less trade war risk)
   - Education, entertainment, healthcare
   - Established business models

**Current Opportunities Found:**
- VIPS (P/E: 6.6) - E-commerce
- TAL (P/E: 11.1) - Education
- FUTU (P/E: 10.5) - Fintech

### **Strategy 4: The "Sector Rotation"**

**Goal:** Find hidden gems in specific growing sectors

1. Pick a promising sector:
   - **Fintech:** Screen emerging markets for TIGR, FUTU, etc.
   - **Education:** Screen China, India for EDU, TAL
   - **Commodities:** Screen Brazil, South Africa
   - **IT Services:** Screen India mid-caps

2. Compare across markets:
   - Is fintech cheaper in China or India?
   - Are Brazilian commodities or South African better value?

3. Build a sector-focused portfolio of small caps

### **Strategy 5: The "Future Index Member"**

**Goal:** Find stocks likely to join major indexes in 2-5 years

**Characteristics to look for:**
- ✅ Market cap $5B-$15B (right size to grow into indexes)
- ✅ Consistent revenue growth > 15% annually
- ✅ Profitable for 3+ consecutive years
- ✅ Meets Graham criteria (quality business)
- ✅ In a growing industry/market

**How to Screen:**
1. Use "Emerging Markets Small Caps"
2. Filter for market cap $5B-$15B
3. Sort by total_score (highest first)
4. Research growth rates
5. These could be future NIFTY 50, BOVESPA 30, etc.

## 💡 Pro Tips for Finding Hidden Gems

### 1. **Focus on Liquid Markets**
Best data quality and tradability:
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ US-listed ADRs (easiest)
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ India .NS/.BO exchanges
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Brazil .SA exchange
- ⭐⭐⭐ China .HK exchange

### 2. **Use Market Cap as a Filter**
| Market Cap | Classification | Potential | Risk |
|------------|----------------|-----------|------|
| < $1B | Micro-cap | 10x-50x | Very High |
| $1B-$10B | Small-cap | 3x-10x | High |
| $10B-$50B | Mid-cap | 2x-5x | Medium |
| > $50B | Large-cap | 1.5x-3x | Lower |

**Sweet spot for "diamonds in the rough":** $1B - $10B

### 3. **Look for Low Analyst Coverage**
- Check how many analysts cover the stock (Google Finance, Yahoo Finance)
- < 5 analysts = very underfo

llowed
- These often have mispricing opportunities

### 4. **Compare to Peers**
- Find the stock's larger competitors
- If VIPS has P/E of 6.6 but BABA has P/E of 17.8, VIPS might be undervalued
- Smaller player at much cheaper valuation = opportunity

### 5. **Watch for Sector Trends**
- Fintech booming? → Screen for small fintech stocks
- Commodities rising? → Screen Brazil/South Africa small caps
- Education growth? → China/India education stocks

### 6. **Check Financial Health First**
Even "hidden gems" need:
- ✅ Positive earnings
- ✅ Current ratio > 1.0
- ✅ Manageable debt
- ✅ Positive cash flow

### 7. **Diversify Across Markets**
Don't put all eggs in one basket:
- 30% India small caps
- 30% China small caps
- 20% Brazil small caps
- 20% Other emerging markets

## ⚠️ Risks of Hidden Gems

### Higher Risk = Higher Potential Reward

**Risks to Consider:**
1. **Liquidity Risk** - Harder to buy/sell large quantities
2. **Information Risk** - Less analyst coverage, harder to research
3. **Volatility** - Bigger price swings (±20-30%)
4. **Business Risk** - Smaller companies can fail
5. **Currency Risk** - For non-USD stocks

**How to Mitigate:**
- Start with small positions (2-5% of portfolio)
- Use US-listed ADRs when possible
- Diversify across 15-20 hidden gems
- Only invest what you can afford to lose
- Use stop losses for individual positions
